Sumgrid privacy policy

Effective 19 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Sumgrid app ("Sumgrid", "the app") uses and why. Sumgrid is made by Venkatesh J M, trading as Twin Lantern Works ("we", "us"). If you have any question, email twinlanternworks@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your progress, streaks, badges and settings are stored only on your phone. We don't receive them.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Information stored on your phone

To save your game, Sumgrid keeps the following on your device: puzzles in progress, solve times, hints and mistakes, daily results, streaks, rewards, badges and your settings (such as sound, vibration, board theme and reminder time). This information stays on your phone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.

Advertising

The free version of Sumgrid shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a small banner on the game screen, an occasional full-screen ad between practice puzzles, and an optional video you can choose to watch for an extra hint.

To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and use information from your device, including your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Whether ads are personalised depends on your choices and the law where you live. Google's use of this information is covered by Google's own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Sumgrid asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads on some phones).

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, Sumgrid schedules a notification on your phone at the time you choose. Reminders are created on your device; no information is sent to us to do this. You can turn reminders off in the app or in Android settings.

Sharing your results

When you tap "Share result", Sumgrid opens your phone's share menu with a short text summary of your solve. Nothing is shared unless you choose an app to send it to.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services that collect personal information.
  • We don't request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location.

Children

Sumgrid is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. Ads in the app are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences with parental guidance. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and want to raise a concern, please contact us.

Security

Your game data stays on your device and is protected by your phone's own security. Ads are delivered over encrypted connections by Google.

Your choices

  • Delete your game data by uninstalling Sumgrid or clearing its storage in Android settings.
  • Manage ad personalisation as described in the Advertising section.
  • Contact us at twinlanternworks@gmail.com with any request or question.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Sumgrid handles information (for example, when in-app purchases are added), we will update this page and change the date at the top.
